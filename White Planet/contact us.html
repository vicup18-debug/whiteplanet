Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can you install after payment?
Usually within 24 to 48 hours for standard residential projects. Large estates may require a scheduled timeline.
Do you install upcountry?
Yes, we have installation partners nationwide. However, our core teams are based in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.
Is the site survey really free?
Yes, site surveys are 100% free for locations within the Lagos and Abuja metropolis.
Can I monitor cameras from my phone?
Absolutely. We configure all systems with a mobile app so you can view live feeds from anywhere in the world.
